# Recursos del login WaveWires

- `underwater.png`: fotografía submarina generada, 1672 × 941.
- `surface.png`: fotografía de superficie generada, 1672 × 941.
- `mark.svg`: símbolo vectorial original construido en código; el nombre WaveWires se compone con texto HTML.

Las dos imágenes se produjeron con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, sin CLI ni clave API, y se copiaron al proyecto conservando los originales. No contienen textos ni controles. WebGL añade el cruce de superficie, la refracción, los rayos móviles y las burbujas; CSS aporta el sombreado para la legibilidad del formulario.

## Prompt final: underwater.png

Create a photorealistic underwater ocean photograph to be used as the full-screen background texture of a professional WaveWires login webpage. Landscape wide 16:9 composition, ideally 2560x1440 or larger. Camera fully submerged a few meters below water, looking diagonally upward at the rippling underside of the sea surface, water ceiling across the upper third, brilliant diffused sunlight entering at upper center-right (65% from left, 8% from top), realistic fine rolling ripples and detailed caustic highlights, soft broad volumetric sunlight shafts filtering down toward the lower half. Rich deep navy blue at left and bottom, luminous ocean cyan at top-right, sophisticated cinematic natural ocean photography, extremely believable water. The left 40% must be darker and calm with generous negative space for a white login form that will be added separately. No seabed visible, no horizon, no fish, no coral, no people, no objects, no bubbles baked in, NO text, NO logo, NO UI, no border, no watermark. Avoid oversharpening, artificial neon, fantasy, excessive god rays. This is a clean background photograph, not a mockup.

## Prompt final: surface.png

Photorealistic ocean surface photograph, wide landscape 16:9 composition. First-person camera at water level, lens almost touching the water, about to sink below surface. Ocean small rolling azure waves fill lower 70% of frame, horizon at upper 30%, pale blue sunlit sky, bright diffused sun at upper center-right (65% from left). Match a clear deep-blue open ocean underwater scene with cyan highlights and navy shadows. Professional cinematic natural photography. Small translucent wavelets close to camera have real texture and reflections, no huge surf, no dramatic storm. Darker navy on left, luminous blue right. No land, boat, person, animal, text, logo, border, watermark, UI, or visible camera. A clean photograph texture for a two-second transition from above water into the ocean; NOT a webpage mockup.
